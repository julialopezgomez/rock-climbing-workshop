Planner Playground

The planner of src/rcr/planner, rebuilt in JavaScript on a 2D wall, with the robot's real reach sets sliced at the trunk's typical distance from the wall (0.127 m). The optimiser is replaced by a toy feasibility rule (tab 5 says which); its clock uses simulated seconds close to the measured ones. Companion to GUIDE.md.

The key takeaway in one line

Eager the search asks the optimiser about every move it considers: thousands of 2–17 s checks.
Ours the search runs on a cheap reach graph (milliseconds a stance), asks the optimiser only about the moves of its best path, and one "no" bans the whole family of moves it rules out.
